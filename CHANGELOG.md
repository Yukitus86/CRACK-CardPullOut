## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-04

### Added
- First release.
- Walking away from a badge reader pulls your ID badge out once the cable goes taut, so you no longer have to press
  **E** to take it back.
- The cable stops holding you back the moment the badge pops out.
- Config file with `Enabled`, `PullMargin`, `TautTime`, `ReleaseImmediately` and `LogEjects`.
